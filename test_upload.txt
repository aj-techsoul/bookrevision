Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the landscape of modern education by introducing personalized learning systems.
These systems analyze student performance in real-time, adapting course content to address individual strengths and weaknesses.
By utilizing AI-powered summarization, students can review lengthy academic papers and textbooks in a fraction of the time.
Furthermore, automated quiz generators can create flashcards and multiple-choice questions dynamically from study notes.
This enables efficient self-assessment, helping learners retain key concepts and improve overall academic performance.
Overall, intelligent learning platforms make high-quality educational resources more accessible to students worldwide.
